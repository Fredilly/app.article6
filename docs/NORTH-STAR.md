# North Star

**Article6 — where we're going and what matters.**

---

## Mission

Make carbon credit verification defensible, traceable, and fast — without losing rigor.

## What we are

A verification factory that gets smarter from every paid review.

## What we are not

- Not "AI for carbon"
- Not a static SaaS tool
- Not a generic AI platform
- Not a consultancy deck with software attached

## The loop

Paid verification → revenue + decision traces → product gets smarter → next verification faster/more defensible → compound.

## Target

- First 5 pilots: $1,500-2,500 per methodology verification
- After 3 case studies with 60-70% time savings: $5K
- VVBs charge $50-100K/engagement — we save them weeks

## Wedge

Forestation/reforestation (ARR) verification for VVBs. One wedge. Own it.

## Scarce asset

High-quality adjudication data from real reviewers on real rules. Not GPUs. Not PDFs. Not prompts. Decision traces.

## Moat

Live verifier workflow access. Decision history. Rule-level evidence. Audit events. Cross-project patterns. Differentiated access to context feedback loops.

## Culture

Truth-seeking. No fake verification. No performative AI. Smallest real improvement. Reviewer trust over flashy UX.

## Key numbers

- ~80 VVBs active in Article 6
- ~20 forestry-focused
- VVBs charge $50-1000K per engagement
- Verification takes weeks manually
- Our target: hours with an auditable trail
